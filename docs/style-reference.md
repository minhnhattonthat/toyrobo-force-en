# Style reference - Toyrobo Force

Use the glossary’s full names, Western name order, hyphenated honorifics, and English ranks. Preserve “Confirm!”, “Teka Revolt,” “Out of Control,” and “field training.” Examples below quote the supplied translation; `/` retains its displayed break notation. The script supports more emotional variety than the glossary’s brief voice labels, especially for Blank.

## Corrected voice example

**dlg:1694 — Tomomi, performing delighted indecision during her apparent recruitment**

- **JP:** トモミ「もしもし、ユウキ？ / あのさー、あたしさー♪」
- **Current EN:** Tomomi: "Hello, Yuki? So, um, I'm, like..."
- **Better EN:** Tomomi: "Hello, Yuki? Sooo... guess what!"
- **Reason:** The current filler makes her sound merely hesitant and introduces a conspicuous “like” tic. The drawn-out vowels and ♪ suggest playful, pleased anticipation. The proposed line carries that performance without prematurely revealing either her appointment or the undercover plan.

Keep the distinction between a character’s claim and the story’s truth: Richter should sincerely sound human before the reveal; Emiko should sincerely sound distressed during her engineered flight; Tomomi’s apparent betrayal must remain playable as convincing enthusiasm. None requires an artificial “robot voice.”
